Delete your account

Matrimonial App - Shaadi (Android) · Developer: KartikRoc

Option 1 — Delete it in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Matrimonial App - Shaadi and sign in.
  2. Go to the 👤 Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete permanently to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2 — Ask us to delete it

If you can no longer open the app, email kartikgupta618@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "Account deletion request". We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, details, date of birth, photos)
  • Interests you sent and received
  • Your chats and messages
  • Your notifications and block list
  • Your sign-in account

What may be kept

  • Reports you filed or that were filed about you, kept for up to 1 year for safety and abuse prevention, then deleted automatically.
