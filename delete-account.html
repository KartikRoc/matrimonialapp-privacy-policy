Delete your account

You can permanently delete your account and personal data at any time. Choose the option that works for you.

Delete in the app Fastest

Takes less than a minute.

  1. Open Matrimonial App - Shaadi and sign in.
  2. Go to the 👤 Profile tab.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  4. Tap Delete permanently to confirm.

✅ Your account and data are deleted immediately, and you are signed out.

Ask us by email

If you can no longer open the app.

  1. Use the Google account you signed in with.
  2. Send an email with the subject "Account deletion request".
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Common questions

Will uninstalling the app delete my account?

No. Uninstalling only removes the app from your phone. Use one of the options above to delete your account and data.

Can I get my account back after deleting it?

No. Deletion is permanent. You can sign up again with a new profile at any time.

Will other members still see my profile?

No. Your profile, photos and chats are removed, so other members can no longer see or contact you.
